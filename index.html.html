About Musketeers Car Wash

The Highest Standard in Auto Detailing On Thika Road

Musketeers Car Wash is built on speed, precision, and complete vehicle care. Located conveniently in Thome right behind Jambo Grill off Thika Road, we cater to individual motorists and commercial fleets seeking top-tier foam washing, undercarriage cleansing, engine degreasing, and interior restoration.

Our yard features structured wash bays, specialized micro-fiber drying technology, high-PSI underbody jets, and a lounge area so you can relax while your car receives treatment.
